Background

The long way round

I started as a software engineer in India — writing code, building systems, learning how enterprise software actually works when real people use it.

That curiosity took me to Italy, then to London in 2005, where I spent the next two decades inside some of the world's largest companies — Unilever, Mars, Shell, BP — leading the kind of transformations that touch hundreds of millions of consumers without any of them knowing it happened.

Programme governance, AI strategy, data platforms, digital marketplaces — I've shipped them all, usually across three continents at once, usually with a budget someone told me was too small.

In 2024 I stepped back from corporate life to build things myself. The gap I kept seeing: AI is genuinely powerful, but almost nobody outside tech is actually using it. That's the problem I'm working on now.
