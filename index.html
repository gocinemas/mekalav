Background

The long way round

I started as a software engineer in India. Writing code, building systems, figuring out how enterprise software actually behaves when real people touch it.

That curiosity took me to Italy, then to London in 2005. Over the next two decades I worked inside some of the world's largest companies. Unilever, Mars, Shell, BP. The kind of transformations that touch hundreds of millions of consumers without any of them knowing it happened.

Programme governance, AI strategy, data platforms, digital marketplaces. I've shipped them all, usually across three continents at once, usually with a budget someone said was too small.

In 2024 I stepped back from corporate life. In 2025 I came back briefly to support the Magnum Ice Cream separation from Unilever, working on the technology side of the M&A programme. Then back to building. The gap I keep seeing: AI is genuinely useful, but almost nobody outside tech is getting the benefit of it. That's the problem I'm working on.

WhatsApp-first AI assistant for everyday life — no app, no login. Built on Python / Flask, Groq, Twilio and Supabase, deployed on Railway.
2026 School Communications ● Live ∨
Connects to school email updates and newsletters. Parents ask questions in plain English — "what time does school finish on Friday?" — and get answers pulled directly from the school's own comms.
2026 My Details — Bill & Renewal Tracker ● Live ∨
Scans your Gmail for energy, broadband, insurance and water bills. Extracts account numbers, renewal dates and prices and imports them into a personal dashboard with one tap.
2026 Book Scanner & Personal Library ● Live ∨
Send a barcode photo on WhatsApp and Miru decodes the ISBN and looks up the book. Tracks your personal library across read, reading and want-to-read. Searchable by title, author or status.
2026 WhatsApp Saves ● Live ∨
Send any URL, photo or idea to save it for later. Retrieve anything with plain-English search: "restaurants I saved last week", "articles about AI". Your personal memory, on WhatsApp.
2026 My Area — Local Services ● Live ∨
Enter a postcode and get a report on your local area: nearest pubs, cafes, schools, pharmacies and parks. Save favourites with address and phone. Powered by Google Places and OpenStreetMap.
2026 Find Cheap Fuel ● Live ∨
Ask "cheapest petrol near KT15" and get a live-ranked list of local pump prices. Covers petrol and diesel across all nearby stations, pulling from the UK government's real-time fuel price API.
2026 Next Train ● Live ∨
Live UK train departures in plain English. "Next train from Woking to Waterloo" returns the next few services with platform, departure time and any delays — no app, no timetable.
2026 Space — UK Space Intelligence ● Live ∨
Everything about UK space — missions, agencies, news, launches and the people behind it — in one place. Ask in plain English, get clear answers. miru.humanagency.co/space →
2024 Your Local Election Results ● Shipped ∨
Built for the May 2024 UK local elections. Enter a postcode, get your ward name, candidates, parties and live results as they came in. Retired after the count.
