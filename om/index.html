What He Built

📰 GigaOm

A blog that became one of the most influential voices in tech. He saw where the industry was heading when others couldn't.

✍️ Writing

Decades of journalism that shaped how the world thinks about technology. Always asking the human questions.

📸 Photography

Landscapes that captured his love of beauty. 100k followers watched him see the world differently.

🤝 People

An investor who believed in founders. A mentor who opened doors. A friend who showed up.

💭 Ideas

The belief that technology serves humanity, not the other way around. That design matters. That kindness is powerful.

❤️ Love

His last word was "love." That says everything about who he was.
